Governed by laws of India

1. Introduction

PROPHUNT LLP ("we", "us", "our", or "PROPHUNT") is a MahaRERA-registered real estate agent (Registration No. A52100037156) with its principal office at Work Katta, 291/3, Pancard Club Rd, Baner, Pune, Maharashtra "“ 411069.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, receive, possess, store, deal with, and handle your personal data when you use our website at prophuntllp.com (the "Website") or engage with our real estate advisory services.

By accessing our Website or submitting your information through any form, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Website or services.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for their marketing purposes. We use your data only to provide and improve our real estate advisory services to you.

4. Sharing of Personal Data

We share your personal data only in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 With Developers (Your Consent Implied)

When you express interest in a specific project, we share your contact details and property preferences with the developer/promoter of that project for the purpose of facilitating a site visit, booking, or further information. By submitting an enquiry for a specific project, you consent to this sharing.

4.2 With Home Loan Partners

If you request home loan assistance, we share your contact information with our empanelled banking and NBFC partners. We will seek your explicit consent before doing so.

4.3 With Legal and Documentation Partners

If you engage our legal assistance service, we share necessary information with our empanelled advocate partners strictly for the purpose of that legal service.

4.4 Legal and Regulatory Disclosure

We may disclose personal data if required by law, court order, or regulatory authority (including MahaRERA), or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

4.5 Service Providers

We use trusted third-party service providers for website hosting, email delivery, analytics, and CRM. These providers are contractually bound to process your data only on our behalf and in accordance with applicable law.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our Website uses cookies "” small text files stored on your device "” to improve your experience. We use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the site (e.g., Google Analytics). These are anonymised and do not identify you personally.
  • Marketing cookies: Used if you have consented to receive relevant advertising (e.g., Facebook Pixel, Google Ads). You may opt out at any time.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling analytics or marketing cookies will not affect core website functionality but may affect our ability to show you relevant property updates.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as is necessary for the purposes described in this Policy:

  • Enquiry and contact data: Up to 3 years from your last interaction with us
  • Transaction-related records: Up to 8 years as required under applicable tax and accounting laws
  • RERA-related records: As required under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Until you unsubscribe
  • Analytics data: Up to 26 months (Google Analytics default)

After the applicable retention period, your data is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (which received Presidential assent on 11 August 2023), you have the following rights as a Data Principal:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and information on how it has been processed.
  • Right to Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal obligations to retain certain records.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to a prompt and effective grievance redressal mechanism for any matter relating to processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Nominate: You may nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Point of Contact:

  • Email: info@prophuntllp.com with subject line "Data Rights Request"
  • Phone: +91 70668 80808
  • Address: PROPHUNT LLP, Work Katta, 291/3, Pancard Club Rd, Baner, Pune "“ 411069

We will respond to all requests within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate your grievance to the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted under the DPDP Act, 2023.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Website and services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data about a minor, please contact us immediately.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all Website communications
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorised team members on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular review of data handling practices
  • Secure disposal of physical documents containing personal information

However, no transmission over the internet or electronic storage method is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

10. Third-Party Links

Our Website contains links to developer websites, MahaRERA portal, Google Maps, and other third-party services. These links are provided for your convenience. PROPHUNT LLP is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the Policy was last revised. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically. Where changes are material, we will notify you via email (if we hold your email address) or by a prominent notice on our Website.
