Common Questions

Questions Buyers Ask Us

Straight answers on how PROPHUNT LLP works, what it costs, and where we operate.

What is PROPHUNT LLP?
PROPHUNT LLP is a RERA-registered real estate channel partner based in Baner, Pune. We help homebuyers and investors discover, evaluate, and purchase premium residential properties from trusted developers like Godrej Properties, Mahindra Lifespaces, and Shapoorji Pallonji across Pune.
Which areas in Pune does PROPHUNT LLP cover?
PROPHUNT LLP covers all major real estate micro-markets in Pune including Baner, Balewadi, Hinjewadi, Wakad, Keshav Nagar, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Baner-Hinjewadi Road, and East Pune. We specialise in premium residential apartments and villa plots.
Is PROPHUNT LLP a RERA registered agent?
Yes, PROPHUNT LLP is a RERA-registered real estate agent operating under the Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority (MahaRERA). All projects we recommend are RERA-verified for buyer protection and transparency.
What developers does PROPHUNT LLP partner with?
PROPHUNT LLP is an authorised channel partner for Godrej Properties, Mahindra Lifespaces, Shapoorji Pallonji, Kohinoor Group, Tejraj Group, VTP Realty, Rohan Builders, and other reputed developers in Pune.
Does PROPHUNT LLP charge buyers any fee?
Not for our core advisory. PROPHUNT LLP's property discovery, project comparison and site-visit advisory is available at no direct cost to buyers, and we are compensated by developers as their authorised channel partner. Buyers pay the same developer price whether they come directly or through us. Optional third-party legal or specialist services may involve separately disclosed fees.
Can NRIs invest in Pune property through PROPHUNT LLP?
Yes. PROPHUNT LLP offers dedicated NRI property investment services for Indians residing abroad. We handle end-to-end coordination including virtual site tours, legal documentation, home loan guidance through NRI-friendly banks, and post-purchase support, all handled remotely.
How do I book a site visit through PROPHUNT LLP?
You can book a free site visit through PROPHUNT LLP by calling +91-70668-80808, emailing info@prophuntllp.com, or clicking 'Book a Site Visit' on our website. Our advisor will schedule a visit at your convenience and accompany you to the project site.
What is the price range of properties listed by PROPHUNT LLP?
PROPHUNT LLP lists properties across a wide budget range: from around ₹74 Lakh for compact apartments (Kalpataru Aria, Karjat) to ₹5.64 Crore+ for premium 4.5 and 5.5 BHK residences (The Balmoral Hillside, Baner). We cater to first-time buyers, upgraders, and investors.
