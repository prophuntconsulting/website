A 60-acre estate built around a lagoon

BluBay is ORA Land’s plotted estate at Haliwali in Karjat, where the land falls away into an open valley. The core 60 acres hold 325 villa plots around a man-made lagoon, with 150 acres of untouched forest beyond.

1.5-acre lagoon

A man-made signature lagoon with a kayak dock, open-air theatre and alfresco seating at its edge.

4.2-acre Miyawaki forest

A dense native forest planted inside the estate, with forest trails, an arboretum and a zen garden.

35,000 sq ft clubhouse

Banquet, wellness spa, gym, pool, business lounge, squash and badminton, under one roof.

85% valley-facing plots

The 60-acre layout follows the contours so most plots look out over the valley.

150 acres of forest around

A permanent belt of untouched forest borders the estate on its far side.

Individual 7/12, NA converted

Each plot comes with its own 7/12 extract and NA conversion, registered with MahaRERA.

BluBay by ORA Land: FAQs

Where is BluBay by ORA Land?

BluBay is at Haliwali, Karjat, Raigad 410201, on the Chowk to Karjat Phata stretch. ORA lists Karjat station about 3 minutes away, Panvel and Navi Mumbai International Airport about 40 minutes, and South Mumbai about 60 minutes via Atal Setu.

What is the price of plots at BluBay?

Villa plots at BluBay start from Rs 74.99 lakh. Plot-wise prices depend on size and position, so ask us for the current price sheet.

What plot sizes are available at BluBay?

BluBay has 325 NA villa plots of 1,500 to 3,500 sq ft across a 60-acre estate.

Is BluBay RERA registered?

Yes. BluBay - Phase 1 is registered with MahaRERA as PP1271012600752, covering 177 plots. You can check it on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.

Are the plots NA with individual 7/12?

Yes. ORA states that every plot is NA converted and comes with its own 7/12 extract.

What amenities does BluBay have?

A 1.5-acre lagoon, a 4.2-acre Miyawaki forest and a 35,000 sq ft clubhouse, with more than 60 amenities across club, sports, lagoon and community zones.

Can NRIs buy a plot at BluBay?

Yes. NRIs and OCIs can buy residential NA plots in India. ORA also offers a virtual presentation for buyers outside Mumbai or India.

Is there a fee for using PROPHUNT LLP?

No. The developer pays our fee, so you pay the developer’s price and nothing extra to us.
