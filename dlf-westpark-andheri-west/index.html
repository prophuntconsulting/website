DLF’s first Mumbai project, now in Phase 2

DLF Westpark is DLF’s first housing project in Mumbai, built with Trident Realty on a 10-acre site in Oshiwara, Andheri West. Phase 1 had 416 homes in four 37-storey towers. All of them were booked within a week of the July 2025 launch.

Phase 2 is a separate MahaRERA registration, The Westpark Phase-1B, granted on 30 October 2026. It adds 3 and 4 BHK homes in Tower 7A.

Questions buyers ask

What is the RERA number of DLF Westpark Phase 2?

PR1181012602591. MahaRERA registered it as “The Westpark Phase-1B” on 30 October 2026, valid until 30 June 2034. The promoter is Westpark Developers Private Limited. Scan the QR code at the foot of this page to open the record on MahaRERA.

What is the address of DLF Westpark Andheri?

Oshiwara, Andheri West, Mumbai 400102. It is about 250 m from Infiniti Mall Andheri and 1.9 km from Lokhandwala Main Street.

What is the price of DLF Westpark Phase 2?

DLF has not published Phase 2 prices. They are shared on request, unit by unit, along with the payment plan. Leave your number and we will send the current sheet.

What are the floor plan sizes in DLF Westpark Phase 2?

The Phase-1B plans filed with MahaRERA show a 3 BHK with 1,261 sq ft RERA carpet area (1,365 sq ft with balcony and dry yard) and a 4 BHK with 2,067 sq ft RERA carpet area (2,171 sq ft in total), both in Tower 7A.

How is Phase 2 different from Phase 1?

Phase 1 (MahaRERA PR1181012500079) had 416 homes in four 37-storey towers and sold out within a week of its July 2025 launch. Phase 2 is a separate registration, PR1181012602591, with new homes in Tower 7A.

Who is developing DLF Westpark?

DLF, in a joint venture with Trident Realty. It is DLF’s first residential project in Mumbai. You may also see it written as DLF West Park.

Can NRIs buy a flat at DLF Westpark?

Yes. NRIs and OCI cardholders can buy residential property in India without RBI approval. Payment has to come through an NRE, NRO or FCNR account or a normal banking remittance, and Indian banks offer home loans to NRIs. We can handle the site visit and paperwork while you are abroad.
