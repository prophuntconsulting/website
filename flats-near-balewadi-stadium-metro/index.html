Flats near Balewadi Metro: Frequently Asked Questions

Which new projects are closest to Balewadi Stadium Metro Station (Line 3)?

By straight-line distance: Greenfinity (about 1.1 km), Rohan Viti (about 1.3 km), Lodha Altero (about 1.6 km), ANP Ultimus (about 1.6 km), Merlin Ventana (about 1.8 km).

What is the price range of flats and homes near Balewadi Metro?

How are the distances on this page measured?

Distances are approximate straight-line distances between each project's map location and Balewadi Stadium Metro Station (Line 3). Actual travel distance by road is longer and depends on route and traffic.
