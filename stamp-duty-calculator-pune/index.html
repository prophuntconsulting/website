  • Registration: 1% of the value, capped at ₹30,000.
  • Value used: the higher of the agreement value and the ready reckoner value.
  • GST: 5% on under-construction homes (1% on affordable housing). None on ready homes with an OC or on resale.
  • TDS: 1% when the price is ₹50 lakh or more. It is deducted from what you pay the seller, not added to it.
