Who We Are

More Than a Brokerage - A Property Partner

PROPHUNT LLP was founded with a clear purpose: to bring honesty and expertise into one of India's most important decisions - buying a home. We saw an industry where clients were overwhelmed by choices, misled by inflated claims, and left without real guidance after the sale.

We built PROPHUNT differently. Our name is inspired by the ancient Sanskrit phrase भूमिं मृगयध्वम् - "Hunt the Land" - a declaration of relentless pursuit on behalf of our clients. We don't stop until we find what's right for you.

Based in Baner, Pune, we work exclusively with RERA-registered projects from developers we have personally evaluated. Every recommendation is made with your interest - not commission - at the centre.

RERA details upfront. We show each project's MahaRERA registration number where available so you can verify it independently.
Post-sale support. Our relationship with clients doesn't end at the agreement - we assist through registration and possession.
No hidden fees. Complete transparency in our service charges and developer pricing.

Why PROPHUNT

What Sets Us Apart

We've built every process around one question: what would be best for our client?

Verified Projects Only

Every project in our portfolio has been independently evaluated: site visits, RERA checks, developer credibility, and delivery track record.

Transparent Pricing

We publish indicative prices and never inflate them. Our advisors explain every cost component (BSP, GST, registration, and maintenance) upfront.

Dedicated Advisor

You get one point of contact throughout your journey. No hand-offs, no call centres. Your advisor is accountable from first enquiry to possession.

NRI Expertise

We have structured processes for NRI buyers: power of attorney, repatriation, FEMA compliance, and virtual site visits with detailed video reports.

Legal Support

Our empanelled legal partners assist with title verification, agreement vetting, stamp duty calculation, and sub-registrar appointments.

Market Intelligence

Access to real-time price data, micro-market trends, and investment return projections for every project we recommend.
