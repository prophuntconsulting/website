Buying plots in Khopoli: what to know

Buying a plot near Khopoli

Plotted projects around Khopoli let you buy land in a gated layout with roads, water lines and common amenities, and build later. They are popular with buyers who want a weekend home or a long-term holding between Mumbai and Pune, especially since the Missing Link opened in 2026.

The checks that matter for land

  • The NA (non-agricultural) order for the layout, the 7/12 extract and the mutation entries.
  • The project’s MahaRERA registration: plotted layouts above the threshold must be registered. Check it yourself.
  • Whether any part of the land is classified as forest or hill slope.
  • The source of water and electricity, and who maintains them after handover.

Who these plots suit

Buyers who want to build a weekend home or a retirement home in a few years, and long-term buyers betting on the Missing Link and the new Navi Mumbai airport bringing more people to the area. Because land earns no rent until you build, it suits buyers who do not need an income from the property soon.
