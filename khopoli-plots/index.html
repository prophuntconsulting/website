Why Khopoli, and why now

For years Khopoli was the quiet town at the foot of the ghat. Three projects have changed that: the Missing Link, Navi Mumbai International Airport and the Atal Setu connector now put it within an hour of both Pune and the new airport.

Open

Missing Link, Mumbai–Pune Expressway

The 13.3 km Missing Link opened on 1 May 2026 and cuts about 25–30 minutes off the ghat section next to Khopoli.

Operating

Navi Mumbai International Airport

Commercial flights began on 25 December 2025. Developers list it at roughly 40–60 minutes from Khopoli by road.

Target Feb 2027

Atal Setu to Expressway connector

MMRDA’s 7.35 km elevated link via Chirle joins Atal Setu (the Mumbai Trans Harbour Link) straight to the Expressway.

Built

IndoSpace logistics parks

Two IndoSpace parks on the Khopoli–Pen Road (17 and 59 acres, about 14.7 lakh sq ft) bring warehousing jobs to Khalapur.

Planned

Virar–Alibaug Multimodal Corridor

Land acquisition is under way; MSRDC says construction could start by March 2027. It is a longer-term catalyst.

Mumbai 3.0 and Khopoli, explained

“Mumbai 3.0” is the name used for the Karnala-Sai-Chirner New Town, a new city that MMRDA is planning across about 323 sq km of Raigad district. It covers 124 villages in the Uran, Panvel and Pen talukas, between Atal Setu and the new airport. Singapore’s Surbana Jurong is drawing up the master plan, and land acquisition began in April 2026.

Khopoli is not one of those 124 villages. It sits further east in Khalapur taluka, where the Mumbai–Pune Expressway meets the ghat. What it shares with Mumbai 3.0 is the new road network: the Atal Setu connector runs through Chirle, about 45 minutes from Khopoli, and the Missing Link now shortens the drive to Pune.

For a plot buyer, that means Khopoli is a plotted, low-density market next to a planned new city, rather than part of it.

Before you buy an NA plot in Khopoli

  1. Check the project approvals. Ask for the approved layout and the project’s registration details, and verify them on the official portal before paying a token amount.
  2. Ask for the NA order and the 7/12 extract. The land must be converted to non-agricultural use for residential plots, and the 7/12 should show the developer or promoter as owner.
  3. Read the title search report. A clear title report from the developer’s lawyer, and your own lawyer’s check, protect you from old claims on the land.
  4. Compare the all-inclusive price. Development charges, club membership, stamp duty and registration add to the plot rate, so compare the final figure.
  5. Visit in the monsoon if you can. Khopoli gets heavy rain; see how the roads, drains and slopes on your plot handle it.

Plots in Khopoli: FAQs

Is Khopoli part of Mumbai 3.0?

No. Mumbai 3.0, officially the Karnala-Sai-Chirner New Town, covers 124 villages in the Uran, Panvel and Pen talukas, with MMRDA as the planning authority. Khopoli is in Khalapur taluka, east of that area. It benefits from the same new roads, the Atal Setu connector via Chirle and the Missing Link, and developers list Atal Setu at Chirle about 45 minutes away.

Which plotted projects are there near Khopoli?

Shapoorji Pallonji Khopoli, a 900+ acre wellness-led plotted destination in pre-launch; NeoLiv Grand Forest, 43 acres near Imagicaa in Khalapur; and Kalpataru Aria on the Karjat-Khopoli Marg. Ask us for current availability and site visits for all three.

What plot sizes are available in Khopoli?

Shapoorji Pallonji's Khopoli plots start from 2,000 sq ft in a 900+ acre destination. NeoLiv Grand Forest offers plots from 1,000 to 2,500 sq ft across 43 acres. Kalpataru Aria offers plots from 1,826 to 6,789 sq ft in an 88-acre gated township.

What is the price of plots in Khopoli?

Shapoorji Pallonji's Khopoli plots start from Rs 89 lakh in pre-launch, and Kalpataru Aria plots from Rs 96 lakh, as published by Kalpataru. NeoLiv Grand Forest plots start from Rs 70 lakh. Prices exclude stamp duty and registration, so compare the all-inclusive figure.

Can NRIs buy NA plots in Khopoli?

Yes. NRIs and OCIs can buy residential NA plots in India. They cannot buy agricultural land or farmhouses, so confirm the plot's NA order and the project's approvals first.

How far is Khopoli from Mumbai and Pune?

NeoLiv lists Pune and Navi Mumbai International Airport at about 60 minutes from its site, Panvel at 35 minutes and Lonavala at 20 minutes. Kalpataru describes Aria as about 90 minutes from Mumbai.
