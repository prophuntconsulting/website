2. Scope of Services

As a MahaRERA-registered real estate agent, PROPHUNT LLP is legally authorised to facilitate the purchase and sale of real estate properties within the State of Maharashtra. Our scope of services includes:

  • Facilitating transactions for RERA-registered real estate projects in Maharashtra
  • Providing property advisory, shortlisting, and negotiation support to buyers
  • Coordinating between prospective purchasers and RERA-registered developers
  • Assisting with documentation guidance (non-legal; not a substitute for independent legal advice)
  • Home loan facilitation through empanelled financial institutions
  • NRI property purchase facilitation in compliance with FEMA 1999

PROPHUNT LLP acts as a facilitating agent and does not itself construct, develop, or sell any real estate project. All projects listed on this website are owned and developed by the respective developers named therein.

3. Our Obligations Under RERA

As a registered real estate agent under Section 9 and Section 10 of RERA, PROPHUNT LLP is bound by the following statutory obligations:

3.1 Facilitation of Registered Projects Only

We are legally permitted to facilitate the purchase or sale of properties only in projects that are registered under MahaRERA. We do not market, promote, or book any project that has not obtained valid MahaRERA registration. All projects listed on this website have been registered under MahaRERA at the time of listing; registration status is subject to change and buyers are advised to independently verify current registration status.

3.2 Disclosure of RERA Registration Number

We are required to and do prominently disclose our MahaRERA Agent Registration Number A52100037156 on all marketing materials, websites, brochures, and communications.

3.3 Not Facilitating Unregistered Transactions

We do not facilitate any booking, sale, purchase, or transfer of any apartment, plot, or building in any project that is not registered under RERA. We do not collect any advance money or booking amount on behalf of any developer for any unregistered project.

3.4 Maintaining Books of Accounts

We maintain complete and accurate books of account, records, and documents for all transactions facilitated. These records are retained for a minimum period as required by law and are available to the Authority upon request.

3.5 Not Engaging in Fraudulent or Unfair Practices

We do not engage in any unfair trade practice or publish any advertisement containing false, misleading, or deceptive representations. All information on this website regarding project specifications, pricing, and completion timelines is sourced from developer disclosures on MahaRERA and is subject to change.

5. Buyer Rights Under RERA

The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 grants significant rights to property buyers. As a RERA-registered agent, we are obligated to inform you of these rights:

  • Right to Information: You are entitled to all information about the project — RERA registration number, sanctioned plans, layout, specifications, carpet area, and completion date — as filed by the developer on MahaRERA.
  • Right to Documents: You are entitled to all documents and information you sought at the time of booking from the developer/promoter.
  • Right Against Delay: If the developer fails to complete or deliver possession by the promised date, you are entitled to compensation, including interest at the applicable rate on amounts paid.
  • Right to Carpet Area Pricing: Developers must quote prices on the basis of carpet area only. No pricing on super built-up area or loading beyond what is permitted.
  • Right Against Defects: If any structural defect or deficiency in service, quality, or provision is found within 5 years of possession, you are entitled to have it rectified without further charge.
  • Right to Cancel: If a developer violates RERA provisions, you have the right to withdraw from the project and receive a full refund with interest.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You may file complaints against the promoter or real estate agent with the MahaRERA Authority or its Adjudicating Officer.

6.1 Internal Grievance

Complaints against registered agents may be filed with the Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority:

  • Online: maharera.mahaonline.gov.in

6.3 Consumer Forum

Consumer complaints may also be filed under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 before the appropriate Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission having jurisdiction.
