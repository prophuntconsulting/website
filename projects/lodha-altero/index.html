Lodha Altero

Wakad, Pune · 3 & 4 BHK Apartments

Lodha Altero is a 3 & 4 BHK residential development by the Lodha Group on the 36-metre-wide Wakad-Bhosari BRTS Road in Wakad, Pune. The project offers 3 BHK, 3 BHK with study, and 4 BHK homes, built around a grand rooftop pool and deck, a hi-tech gymnasium, a dedicated padel court, and a multipurpose sports court for basketball, volleyball and tennis.
