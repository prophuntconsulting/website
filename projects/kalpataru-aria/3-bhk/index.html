Kalpataru Aria is an 88-acre gated township at the foothills of the Sahyadris in Karjat, designed by Padma Bhushan architect Hafeez Contractor with landscaping by Green Space Alliance. The retreat-style address offers 2 BHK homes starting at ₹74.49 Lakhs, hill-view plots from 1,615 to 9,000 sqft starting at ₹88 Lakhs, and spacious 3 BHK luxury townhouses (1,700–1,800 sqft carpet) starting at ₹2.01 Cr. Residents get access to 30+ lifestyle amenities including a Grand Health Club, with excellent connectivity via the Mumbai–Pune Expressway, the upcoming Navi Mumbai International Airport, and Atal Setu - part of Karjat's fast-emerging Mumbai 3.0 growth corridor. Backed by Kalpataru's 53+ year legacy of over 110 landmark projects across Mumbai, Thane and Pune.
