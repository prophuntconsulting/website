Expert Negotiation

Secure the best pricing, payment plans, and freebies on your behalf.

Included

Why Negotiation Matters

Developer pricing is rarely fixed. As authorised channel partners, we have direct relationships with project sales teams and a detailed understanding of project-level pricing corridors. This gives us room to negotiate in ways an individual buyer cannot.

  • Base selling price (BSP) negotiation with developer CRM teams
  • Flexible payment plan options: construction-linked, down-payment, subvention schemes
  • Freebies and add-ons: car parks, club membership, modular fittings
  • Floor-selection strategy based on price-to-value analysis
  • Launch vs. resale pricing comparison to identify best entry point

Transparency You Can Count On

We share the complete cost breakup with you: BSP + floor rise + PLC + GST + registration + maintenance deposit. No hidden charges, no "surprise costs at the time of booking."

Legal & Documentation

End-to-end legal support from RERA check to registration.

Advisory Fees Apply

Legal Due Diligence

Buying a property in India involves a complex legal ecosystem. Our empanelled legal partners provide comprehensive due diligence support to protect your investment.

  • RERA registration verification for every project
  • Title search and encumbrance certificate review
  • Agreement for Sale (AFS) vetting, identifying unfair clauses
  • Stamp duty and registration charges calculation (Pune sub-registrar rates)
  • Sub-registrar appointment scheduling and accompaniment
  • Society / OC / CC status verification at possession stage

We recommend independent legal review for every transaction above ₹50 Lakh. Our empanelled advocates charge a flat fee starting at ₹15,000 for full documentation review.

Documents We Help You Manage

Allotment letter, builder-buyer agreement, demand letters, payment receipts, possession letter, OC/CC, share certificate, and society formation documents.

Investing in Pune Real Estate

Pune's residential market has delivered consistent appreciation over the past decade, particularly in the western corridor (Baner, Balewadi, Hinjewadi) driven by IT and manufacturing employment. Our advisors provide the data to make informed investment decisions.

NRI Services

Dedicated support for Non-Resident Indians investing in Pune real estate.

Dedicated Desk

Buying Property as an NRI

India's real estate rules for NRI buyers have specific requirements under FEMA 1999 and the Foreign Exchange Management (Acquisition and Transfer of Immovable Property in India) Regulations. Our dedicated NRI desk is experienced in navigating these requirements.

  • FEMA compliance guidance for property purchase and repatriation
  • OCI / PIO documentation support
  • Power of Attorney (PoA) drafting and registration assistance
  • Virtual site visits with video walkthroughs and detailed reports
  • NRI home loan coordination with India-based bank branches
  • Rental management referrals post-possession
  • Repatriation of sale proceeds guidance (FEMA Schedule I)

All consultations for NRI clients are available via video call. We provide a single point of contact who handles all coordination with developers, banks, and legal teams on your behalf.
