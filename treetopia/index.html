About Shapoorji Pallonji Treetopia

Shapoorji Pallonji Treetopia by SPRE redefines premium plotted living along Hadapsar–Saswad Road, East Pune. Spread across a vast 105-acre hillside landscape, this gated development offers premium NA villa plots with individual 7/12 extracts — a rare and legally robust offering in the Jadhavwadi micro-market.

Designed around nature, Treetopia features over 60% open and green spaces, a 25-acre natural reservoir, scenic hill views, and a well-planned internal layout with wide spine roads and a dedicated lakeside promenade. A luxury clubhouse set on a 3-acre parcel anchors the lifestyle experience.

Strategically located, SPRE Treetopia enjoys strong connectivity to Hadapsar, Magarpatta City, Koregaon Park, and Pune's prominent IT hubs. Surrounded by 600+ acres of government forest and close to Fort Jadhavgadh, this project offers a rare balance of tranquillity, accessibility, and future-ready value under one of India's most trusted real estate brands.
