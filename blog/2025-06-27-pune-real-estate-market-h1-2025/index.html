Pune Residential Market H1 2025: Price Trends, Top Micro-Markets & Outlook

Baner, Hinjewadi and Balewadi recorded 8–12% year-on-year price appreciation in H1 2025. Here is what the data says and where value lies today.

Back to Blog

Overview

The first half of 2025 has reinforced Pune's position as one of India's most resilient residential real estate markets. Demand remained strong across the city's western corridor, driven by continued IT sector hiring, infrastructure upgrades, and a growing preference for developer-backed projects with RERA compliance.

Key Highlights — H1 2025

  • 8–12% YoY price appreciation across Baner, Hinjewadi Road, and Balewadi
  • 3 BHK configurations remain the most in-demand, accounting for 58% of new bookings in premium projects
  • Villa plots in East Pune (Jadhavwadi, Wagholi) saw renewed interest from investors post-Shapoorji Pallonji's Treetopia launch
  • NRI enquiries increased by 22% compared to H1 2024, with the US and UK segments leading

Micro-Market Breakdown

Baner – Hinjewadi Road

The prime corridor for IT professionals. Average prices have moved from ₹9,200/sqft to ₹10,100/sqft. Godrej River Royale's launch has set a new benchmark at ₹12,500+/sqft for branded luxury.

Pimpri-Chinchwad

Emerging as a strong value pick. Mahindra Citadel at Pimpri is attracting first-time buyers with 2 BHK pricing starting under ₹1 Cr and strong social infrastructure.

East Pune (Keshav Nagar, Wagholi)

Budget-friendly corridor seeing developer attention. Godrej Aqua Vista at Keshav Nagar offers 2 & 3 BHK from ₹96.99 Lakh, appealing to end-users and investors alike.

What Should Buyers Do?

If you have been waiting to buy, waiting further is unlikely to benefit you — prices in the western corridor have a clear upward bias, and inventory in RERA-verified projects from branded developers tends to tighten quickly after launch pricing.

Our recommendation: shortlist now, visit within 30 days. The pre-launch window for several upcoming Baner projects closes in Q3 2025.
