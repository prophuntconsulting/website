Luxury Apartments in Pune: Complete Buyer's Guide 2026

PH
PROPHUNT LLP 29 June 2026 · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Explore luxury apartments in Pune with expert insights on top locations, prices, amenities, investment potential, and smart buying tips from PropHunt LLP.

Introduction

Luxury Apartments in Pune have become one of the most preferred choices among homebuyers, professionals, NRIs, and investors seeking premium lifestyles with long-term value. Pune's thriving IT industry, world-class infrastructure, and rapidly developing residential hubs have created strong demand for upscale homes that combine comfort, convenience, and investment potential.

However, choosing the right luxury apartment involves much more than selecting an attractive project. Buyers must evaluate location, developer credibility, amenities, legal approvals, and future appreciation before making a decision.

At PropHunt LLP, we help buyers identify RERA-registered premium projects that match their lifestyle and investment goals while ensuring complete transparency throughout the buying journey.

Why Luxury Apartments in Pune Are in High Demand

The demand for luxury apartments continues to grow because buyers today expect more than just four walls. They seek modern lifestyles, better security, wellness facilities, and premium communities.

Major reasons behind this growing demand include:

  • Rapid expansion of Pune's IT and business sectors
  • High-income professionals relocating to Pune
  • Excellent educational institutions
  • Better connectivity through metro expansion
  • Growing demand from NRIs
  • Strong rental income opportunities
  • Increasing appreciation in premium micro-markets

Luxury residences also provide enhanced privacy, smart home technology, landscaped open spaces, and lifestyle amenities that improve overall quality of life.

Best Locations for Luxury Apartments in Pune

Baner

Baner remains one of Pune's fastest-growing premium residential destinations. Excellent connectivity, IT parks, restaurants, and social infrastructure make it ideal for professionals and families.

Why buy here

  • Excellent appreciation potential
  • Close to Hinjewadi IT Park
  • Premium residential communities
  • Strong rental demand

Browse luxury projects in Baner, the 4 BHK flats in Baner and the larger residences on Pan Card Club Road, such as The Balmoral Hillside, 24K Altura and Lodha Massimo.

Balewadi

Balewadi offers a perfect balance between luxury living and urban convenience. Wide roads, sports infrastructure, shopping destinations, and upcoming developments continue to increase property values.

Ideal for:

  • IT professionals
  • Investors
  • Young families

See the projects within about 3 km of Balewadi High Street.

Kharadi

Kharadi has transformed into one of Pune's largest employment hubs. Premium residential developments continue attracting buyers looking for both end-use and investment.

Key advantages:

  • EON IT Park
  • World-class commercial infrastructure
  • Excellent rental yields
  • Metro connectivity

Wakad

Wakad offers premium apartments at comparatively competitive prices while maintaining excellent connectivity to Hinjewadi, Baner, and Pimpri-Chinchwad.

Popular among:

  • First-time luxury buyers
  • Working professionals
  • Investors

Compare 3 BHK and 4 BHK flats in Wakad, or read our Baner vs Wakad vs Hinjewadi vs Tathawade comparison.

Koregaon Park

Known for its upscale lifestyle, Koregaon Park remains one of Pune's most prestigious residential addresses.

Highlights include:

  • Premium social infrastructure
  • High-end restaurants
  • Boutique retail
  • Excellent resale value

Price Range & Investment Potential

Luxury apartment prices in Pune vary depending on location, builder reputation, project specifications, and connectivity.

Indicative price ranges (they vary widely by project and change often, so always ask for a current cost sheet):

  • Baner: ₹1.2 Cr to ₹3 Cr+
  • Balewadi: ₹1 Cr to ₹2.8 Cr+
  • Kharadi: ₹90 Lakhs to ₹2.5 Cr+
  • Wakad: ₹90 Lakhs to ₹2 Cr+
  • Koregaon Park: ₹2 Cr to ₹8 Cr+

For current published prices, see each project page: for example ANP Privado on Pan Card Club Road and Venkatesh Tresor in Pallod Farms, Baner.

Premium projects in established locations generally experience better capital appreciation and consistent rental demand compared to ordinary residential developments.

Key Things to Check Before Buying

Before purchasing a luxury apartment, buyers should verify:

  • RERA registration, on the official MahaRERA portal
  • Builder reputation (see our developer pages)
  • Title clearance
  • Construction quality
  • Carpet area
  • Maintenance charges
  • Future infrastructure developments
  • Possession timeline
  • Home loan approvals
  • Location connectivity

Taking professional guidance during these checks helps reduce investment risks.

Frequently asked questions

Are luxury apartments in Pune a good investment?

They can be, for the right project and holding period. Returns depend on the exact location, the developer’s delivery record, the price you pay and how long you hold, so compare the complete cost sheet and the project’s MahaRERA details rather than relying on general market claims.

Which area is best for luxury apartments in Pune?

Baner (including Pan Card Club Road), Balewadi, Kharadi, Wakad and Koregaon Park are among the most sought-after premium addresses. The best fit depends on where you work, the schools you need and your budget.

What should buyers check before booking a luxury apartment?

Verify the MahaRERA registration on the official portal, the developer’s track record, title and approvals, carpet area, the complete cost sheet including taxes and charges, maintenance costs and the possession timeline.
