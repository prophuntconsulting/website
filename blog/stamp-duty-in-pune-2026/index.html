Stamp Duty in Pune 2026: Registration Charges, GST and What You Actually Pay

PH
PROPHUNT Advisory Team 23 October 2026 · 6 min read

Stamp duty, metro cess, registration fees, GST and TDS for a home in Pune in 2026, explained with a worked example for a ₹1 crore flat and the other costs to budget for.

The price on a brochure is never the full cost of a home. In Pune, stamp duty, registration and GST alone can add more than 12% to the agreement value of a new flat. This guide explains stamp duty in Pune in 2026, along with registration charges, how they are calculated, and what a buyer actually pays, with a worked example. To run the numbers for your own flat, use our free Pune stamp duty calculator.

Stamp duty in Pune: the current rates

Stamp duty in Maharashtra is set under the Maharashtra Stamp Act and collected by the Department of Registration and Stamps, known as IGR Maharashtra. Inside Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) and Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC) limits, the commonly applied rates are:

BuyerStamp dutyMetro cessLocal body surchargeTotal
Man, alone or with other men5%1%1%7%
Woman buying alone or with other women4%1%1%6%

A few points to know:

  • The metro cess of 1% applies to sale deeds in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad, as it does in Mumbai and Nagpur.
  • The women's concession of 1% has applied since April 2021 to residential property bought by a woman alone or with other women.
  • Joint ownership between a man and a woman is where guides disagree. Some quote 6.5%, others the full 7%. Confirm the rate for your exact ownership structure with the sub-registrar before you budget.
  • Outside municipal limits, for example in many gram panchayat areas around Pune, the rate is lower. Check the rate for the exact village with IGR.

Stamp duty in Pune can change with a state budget, so always check the current schedule on the IGR site before you pay.

Registration charges

The registration fee for a property sale in Maharashtra is 1% of the property value, capped at ₹30,000. For almost any flat in Pune above ₹30 lakh, that means a flat ₹30,000.

Registration happens at the sub-registrar's office (or through the e-registration route where the developer offers it), and both buyer and seller, or their authorised representatives, must be present.

What value is stamp duty charged on?

Stamp duty is charged on the higher of two values:

  1. the agreement value, the price written in your agreement for sale, or
  2. the ready reckoner value, the government's minimum value for that area, published by IGR as the Annual Statement of Rates.

For a new flat from a developer, the agreement value is usually higher, so that is what you pay on. For resale flats or plots priced below the ready reckoner rate, duty is still charged on the ready reckoner value. You can look up the rates for any area through the IGR Maharashtra portal. Maharashtra kept the ready reckoner rates unchanged for 2026-27, so the rates revised in April 2025 still apply.

GST: only on under-construction homes

GST is a separate cost from stamp duty and applies only to homes bought before the occupancy certificate (OC) is issued:

  • 5% GST (without input tax credit) on the price of a regular under-construction home
  • 1% GST on an affordable home, which means a price of up to ₹45 lakh and a carpet area of up to 90 square metres in a city like Pune
  • No GST on a ready-to-move home where the OC has already been issued, or on a resale home

This is one reason ready homes can be more cost-effective than they first look. Compare the full cost, not just the base price.

TDS: deducted, not added

If the property costs ₹50 lakh or more, the buyer must deduct 1% TDS from the payment to the seller and deposit it with the income tax department. From April 2026 this falls under section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, which replaced the old section 194-IA. The rate and threshold are unchanged.

TDS is not an extra cost. It comes out of the amount you pay the seller, but you are responsible for depositing it and filing the return on time.

Worked example: a ₹1 crore under-construction flat in PMC

Assume an agreement value of ₹1 crore, above the ready reckoner value, for a flat that is not affordable housing:

CostMale buyerWoman buyer
Agreement value₹1,00,00,000₹1,00,00,000
Stamp duty (7% / 6%)₹7,00,000₹6,00,000
Registration (capped)₹30,000₹30,000
GST at 5%₹5,00,000₹5,00,000
Total on top of the price₹12,30,000₹11,30,000

TDS of ₹1,00,000 is deducted from the price you pay the developer, so it is not included above. If the same flat were ready to move, with an OC, the GST line would disappear and the extra cost would fall to about ₹7.3 lakh.

To try a different price, buyer or construction status, use the stamp duty calculator. It also handles a ready reckoner value that is higher than the agreement value.

Other costs to budget for

These vary by project, so ask for each one in writing before you book:

  • maintenance deposit, often collected for one or two years in advance
  • society formation and share money
  • electricity meter and water connection charges
  • car parking, where it is sold separately
  • floor-rise and preferential location charges
  • legal and documentation fees
  • home loan processing fees, if you take a loan

A complete cost sheet from the developer should list every one of these. Our Baner vs Wakad vs Hinjewadi vs Tathawade guide explains why comparing projects on the full cost, rather than the rate per sq ft, matters.

How to keep the cost down legally

  • Consider registering in a woman's name if it suits your family's plans. The saving is 1% of the value.
  • Compare ready and under-construction options. A ready home with an OC avoids GST entirely.
  • Check whether the project qualifies as affordable housing. The GST rate drops from 5% to 1%.
  • Negotiate the agreement value, not just the extras. Stamp duty and GST are percentages, so every lakh off the price also cuts the taxes.

Before booking, also check the project's MahaRERA registration so you know exactly which promoter and which phase you are paying for.

Frequently asked questions

What is the stamp duty in Pune in 2026?

Inside PMC and PCMC limits the commonly applied total is 7% for men (5% stamp duty, 1% metro cess and 1% local body surcharge) and 6% for women buying alone or with other women. Confirm the current rate for your ownership structure on the IGR Maharashtra portal.

How much are registration charges in Pune?

The registration fee is 1% of the property value, capped at ₹30,000, so for most flats in Pune it is ₹30,000.

Is GST payable on a ready-to-move flat?

No. GST applies only to homes bought before the occupancy certificate is issued: 5% for a regular home and 1% for affordable housing. A ready home with an occupancy certificate, or a resale home, has no GST.

Is stamp duty charged on the agreement value or the ready reckoner rate?

On the higher of the two. For most new flats the agreement value is higher; for resale homes or plots priced below the ready reckoner rate, duty is charged on the ready reckoner value.
