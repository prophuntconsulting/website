About

Shapoorji Pallonji

Founded in 1865, the Shapoorji Pallonji Group is one of India’s most trusted names in construction and real estate. Its legacy spans iconic landmarks, large-scale infrastructure and thoughtfully planned communities built on quality, transparency and timely delivery. India’s First Wellness Capital brings that legacy to a wellness-led plotted destination near Khopoli.

16 group companies · 37,000+ workforce. Landmarks built by the Group include Bharat Mandapam, BAPS Mandir (Abu Dhabi), the RBI building (Mumbai), Chenab Bridge and Chenani–Nashri Tunnel (J&K), Atal Tunnel (H.P.), The Imperial Towers (Tardeo) and SP Infocity (Pune).
