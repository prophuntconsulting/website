Viceroy Visava, Andheri West

3 and 4 BHK residences in a single tower of 83 homes in Versova.

About Viceroy Visava

Viceroy Visava is a single residential tower by Viceroy Properties in Versova, Andheri West, registered with MahaRERA under PR1180002502241. It has only 83 residences, with the first habitable floor at about 110 ft.

Every home has French windows and an 8 ft deep private deck, with finished ceiling heights of about 10.3 ft. Amenities include a heated swimming pool and jacuzzi, garden lawn and walking track, yoga room and meditation deck, spa and wellness room, and a terrace pavilion.
