How to compare launches in Andheri West

  1. Compare RERA carpet area, not the brochure size. Carpet area is what MahaRERA registers and what the agreement for sale states.
  2. Match the possession date to your move. Kalpataru Vian’s current RERA timeline is June 2032; check each project’s committed date on MahaRERA.
  3. Ask for the all-inclusive price sheet. Stamp duty, registration and GST add to the base price, so compare the final figure.
  4. Visit at the time of day you will live there. Traffic on Link Road and the Lokhandwala back road differs a lot between morning and evening.

Andheri West new projects: FAQs

Which new residential projects are launching in Andheri West?

Three new projects with MahaRERA registration and published unit details are DLF The Westpark Phase 2 in Oshiwara, Kalpataru Vian on Lokhandwala Back Road, and Viceroy Visava in Versova. Other launches in the area are still awaiting MahaRERA registration.

How do I check a project's MahaRERA registration?

Search the registration number on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in. The record shows the promoter, the registered carpet areas, the approved plans and the completion date the developer has committed to.

Which project suits a 3 BHK buyer in Andheri West?

Compare the RERA carpet area, not the marketing size, and the possession date against when you need to move.
