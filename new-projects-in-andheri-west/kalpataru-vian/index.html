Kalpataru Vian, Andheri West

3, 4 and 4.5 BHK residences on Lokhandwala Back Road, beside the mangroves.

About Kalpataru Vian

Kalpataru Vian is a new launch by Kalpataru on an approximately 4-acre corner plot on Lokhandwala’s back road in Andheri West, next to the mangroves. It is registered with MahaRERA as “Kalpataru Hrushikesh” (PR1180002600863).

The project offers 3, 4 and 4.5 bedroom residences with large decks, and Kalpataru lists more than 30 amenities across lifestyle, fitness, sports and community spaces, including a swimming pool, outdoor jacuzzi, gym, yoga and pilates studio, pickleball and badminton courts, a banquet hall and a co-working lounge.
