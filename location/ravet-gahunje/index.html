FAQs

Ravet-Gahunje — Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ravet-Gahunje located?

Ravet and Gahunje sit along the Old Mumbai-Pune Highway on the northern edge of Pimpri-Chinchwad, within reach of Wakad and Hinjewadi.

What is Gahunje known for?

Gahunje is home to the Maharashtra Cricket Association Stadium, and the broader Ravet-Gahunje belt has seen growing residential development along the highway corridor.

How connected is this area to the IT corridor?

The Ravet flyover and Old Mumbai-Pune Highway connect the area to Wakad and onward to Hinjewadi’s IT parks, making it a relevant option for buyers priced out of the core corridor.
