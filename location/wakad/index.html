Area guide

Living in Wakad: Area Guide

Wakad at a glance

Wakad lies in West Pune, between the Hinjewadi IT parks and the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway, and falls under the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC). Its location, a short drive from Hinjewadi with quick highway access, made it one of the city’s fastest-growing residential areas, with large gated projects, the Phoenix Mall of the Millennium and a wide choice of schools and hospitals.

Prices: a pause after a strong run

Knight Frank India’s H1 2026 report recorded Wakad at about ₹12,115 to ₹12,588 per sq ft, unchanged over 12 months, after a strong 2025. Heavy new supply in and around Wakad is being absorbed, which gives buyers more choice and more room to negotiate than a year ago. It remains well below Baner, at about ₹16,418 to ₹16,987.

Connectivity

Wakad’s draw is its position: the Hinjewadi IT parks on one side, the highway towards Mumbai, Bengaluru and the rest of Pune on the other. Pune Metro Line 3 has a Wakad Chowk station in its first section, from Maan to Balewadi. The flip side is traffic: Bhumkar Chowk and the Wakad flyover are among Hinjewadi’s best-known choke points at peak hours.

Who Wakad suits

  • IT professionals working in Hinjewadi who want an established neighbourhood rather than a newer one further out.
  • Families who want malls, schools and hospitals close by at a lower price than Baner.
  • Buyers weighing it against its neighbour: see our Tathawade vs Wakad comparison.

What to check

  • Water. PCMC cut supply in 2026 when the Pavana dam ran low, and many Wakad societies rely partly on tankers. Ask any project how it is supplied and what the society spends on tankers.
  • Monsoon flooding. Heavy rain in 2026 waterlogged roads in Wakad and closed underpasses towards Tathawade and Punawale. Check the approach roads.
  • Traffic. Drive the route to work at peak hours, especially through Bhumkar Chowk.

FAQs

Wakad — Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Wakad located and what are its key advantages?

Wakad is a prominent suburb in Pune, Maharashtra, close to the Mumbai-Pune Expressway. Its biggest advantage is proximity to the Hinjewadi IT Park (Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park), making it a preferred residential choice for IT professionals, with good connectivity to Pimpri-Chinchwad as well.

How is the connectivity and public transport in Wakad?

Wakad has strong public transport via the Wakad and Hinjewadi bus stands, and connectivity is set to improve further with the under-construction Metro line extending towards Hinjewadi. The Mumbai-Pune Expressway and Aundh-Ravet Road provide smooth travel to the rest of Pune.

What kind of lifestyle does Wakad offer residents?

Wakad offers a self-sufficient, modern lifestyle with shopping malls, multiplexes and restaurants alongside established educational institutions and healthcare facilities, making it popular with both young professionals and families.

Are there prominent developers active in Wakad?

Wakad has attracted several established developers building apartments and mixed-use projects, reflecting continued builder confidence in the micro-market’s long-term demand.

Did Wakad property prices rise in 2026?

No. Knight Frank India recorded Wakad at about ₹12,115 to ₹12,588 per sq ft in H1 2026, unchanged over 12 months after a strong 2025, as heavy new supply was absorbed.

Is Wakad under PMC or PCMC?

Wakad falls under the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC), which handles its water supply, roads and property tax.
